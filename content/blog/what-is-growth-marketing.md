Growth marketing is a data-driven approach to acquiring, retaining and monetizing customers through iterative, measurable testing across channels. It borrows its discipline from four places: Agile marketing's short cycles, the Lean Startup's build-measure-learn loop, Scrum's structure, and performance marketing's obsession with numbers.

## What makes it "growth" marketing instead of just marketing

Four principles separate growth marketing from a standard campaign calendar:

- **Experimentation.** Every initiative starts as a test, not a bet.
- **Scalability.** Only tests that can run wider get more budget.
- **Measurability.** Every channel reports to the same metrics: CAC, LTV, conversion rate.
- **Sustainability.** Wins have to survive past the initial spike, or they don't count.

## The four methods it borrows from

**Agile marketing** runs campaigns in short, cross-functional cycles with a retrospective at the end of each one, so the next cycle starts smarter than the last.

**Lean Startup principles** show up as Minimum Viable Campaigns: a single channel, a small budget, a fixed time box, run as a build-measure-learn loop instead of a full launch.

**Scrum** gives the process its structure: a growth lead running point like a Scrum Master, the client or stakeholder as Product Owner, time-boxed sprints, and a review at the end of each one.

**Performance marketing** supplies the scoreboard: CAC, ROAS and LTV, tracked in real time so a test gets killed or scaled based on numbers, not opinions.
