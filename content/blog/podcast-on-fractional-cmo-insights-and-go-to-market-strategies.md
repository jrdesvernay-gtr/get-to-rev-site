- Using agile marketing to shape a go-to-market plan on a tight budget
- What actually changes when you bring in a fractional GTM engineer instead of hiring
- Building a lean marketing roadmap instead of a 12-month plan nobody revisits
- Connecting product development to growth early, instead of bolting growth on after launch
