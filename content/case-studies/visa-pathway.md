## The mission

Visa Pathway is a boutique immigration consulting firm specializing in work visas for high-skilled professionals relocating to the U.S. and Canada. It provides personalized services through the complex visa process.

Visa Pathway was struggling to capture qualified leads for high-skilled visa prospects through its existing channels, particularly AdWords, which was underperforming. It also lacked a strong content presence and needed to explore new channels like LinkedIn to reach its audience.

## The work

In four months, Get-To-Rev executed a comprehensive multi-channel strategy. Month 1 launched targeted LinkedIn outreach, revamped AdWords campaigns, and video content on YouTube and Instagram. By month 2, these efforts were optimized, refining messaging and content based on performance data.

In month 3, campaigns scaled to new audiences and industries, with the content strategy growing through in-depth articles and SEO. By month 4, Visa Pathway saw significant increases in qualified leads, website traffic and brand engagement, positioning it as a trusted expert in immigration consulting.

With a 180% increase in high-quality leads and a 230% growth in website traffic, Visa Pathway is well-positioned for long-term growth.

### Onboarding

- **First online meeting.** Understood the target audience (high-skilled professionals seeking work visas) and pain points: low AdWords performance, scattered content, and a need for effective email marketing.
- **Package suggestion.** Revamping marketing channels, developing email campaigns, and centralizing content management.
- **Roadmap.** LinkedIn prospecting, a revamped AdWords campaign, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, a Uberflip-managed content strategy, Mailchimp email marketing, and Reddit advertising.

### First month: roadmap implementation

- **LinkedIn prospecting.** Targeted outreach to high-skilled professionals in tech, healthcare and engineering, with personalized connection requests and follow-ups.
- **AdWords revamp.** Refined keywords and ad copy for U.S. and Canadian work-visa searches, with adjusted bidding to lower costs while raising conversions.
- **YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.** 30-60 second videos on visa tips and immigration-law updates, building authority and engagement.
- **Mailchimp.** A segmented email campaign with visa tips, process updates and consultation calls-to-action.
- **Content management with Uberflip.** Centralized distribution of blog articles, videos and guides across channels.
- **Reddit advertising.** Sponsored posts on immigration-related threads, driving traffic to the site and blog.

### Second month and beyond

LinkedIn messaging was refined around specific industries and Visa Pathway's success rate. AdWords kept optimizing toward high-conversion phrases like "work visa Canada" and "skilled worker visa." Video content was adjusted based on performance and FAQ themes from social media and Reddit. Content clusters expanded via Uberflip with SEO and internal linking improvements, and email and Reddit campaigns were refined based on engagement data.

### Third and fourth months: scaling

LinkedIn outreach expanded into finance and healthcare, with free consultation offers and visa assessment tools added. AdWords added retargeting for non-converting visitors. A new video series featured specific visa scenarios and client success stories. Content marketing expanded with long-form articles and guest contributions, email segments nurtured high-value leads, and Reddit ads expanded into niche subreddits for tech careers and expat communities.

## Conclusion

With a 180% increase in high-quality leads and a 230% growth in website traffic, Visa Pathway is well-positioned for long-term growth, continuing to benefit from the strategic marketing efforts implemented by Get-To-Rev.

## Results

- **180% increase in high-quality leads** from LinkedIn outreach, revamped AdWords and email marketing.
- **230% growth in website traffic** from content marketing, Reddit ads and SEO.
- **40% decrease in cost-per-acquisition** from improved targeting, email nurturing and SEO.
- **10K+ YouTube and Instagram views** across the Shorts and Reels series.
- **Strong Reddit engagement**, with a high number of inquiries from immigration-related threads.
