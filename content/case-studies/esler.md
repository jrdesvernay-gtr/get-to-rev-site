## The mission

ESLer is an e-learning platform that provides interactive, online English courses aimed at helping Asian students in the U.S. and Canada improve their language skills. Its marketing needed to focus on reaching parents, who are typically the decision-makers for their children's education.

ESLer needed a way to directly reach parents of Asian students who wanted to improve their children's English proficiency but struggled with brand awareness and engagement. It lacked a comprehensive digital strategy to reach this key demographic and convert them into customers.

## The work

Over three months, Get-To-Rev's tailored marketing strategy helped ESLer successfully reach parents of Asian students in the U.S. and Canada, significantly increasing enrollments.

In the first month, Get-To-Rev launched AdWords campaigns, Facebook and Instagram ads, TikTok influencer collaborations, and sponsored Reddit posts. By the second month, YouTube ads were added, expanding ESLer's reach and driving higher engagement. Continuous optimization through A/B testing and targeting adjustments resulted in a 150% increase in enrollments and a 200% boost in website traffic by the end of the third month.

### Onboarding

- **First online meeting.** Understood ESLer's goals and target audience: parents of Asian students in the U.S. and Canada seeking quality English education for their children.
- **Package suggestion.** Market research plus a multi-channel digital strategy focused on reaching parents.
- **Roadmap.** Campaigns launched across the channels parents actually engage with.

### First month: roadmap implementation

- **AdWords.** Search and display ads optimized for parents seeking English courses for their children.
- **Facebook and Instagram.** Creative featuring student-success testimonials, targeted at parents in the U.S. and Canada.
- **TikTok influencer campaign.** Videos showcasing student success, shareable by children with their parents, raising awareness indirectly among decision-makers. An education- and family-focused influencer was recruited to demonstrate ESLer's benefit in a way parents could see.
- **Reddit sponsored posts.** Placed in parent-focused subreddits and education communities, directing traffic to the platform.

### Second month and beyond

Campaigns were optimized with A/B testing on creative and targeting, with budget shifted toward Facebook and Instagram where parent engagement was highest. YouTube ads launched with short, informative videos featuring real parent success stories. The multi-channel strategy continued to expand ESLer's visibility among parents of Asian students, with influencer engagement on TikTok maintained to appeal to both students and parents.

## Conclusion

Get-To-Rev's focused digital marketing approach allowed ESLer to successfully reach parents of Asian students in North America. Through multi-channel campaigns, ESLer increased enrollments and established itself as a trusted brand for English-language education.

**Ongoing partnership.** ESLer continues to work with Get-To-Rev for ongoing campaign optimization, ensuring its marketing remains effective as it scales.

## Results

- **150% increase in enrollments**, driven by parents of Asian students in the U.S. and Canada within the first two months.
- **200% increase in website traffic** from Google AdWords and social ads targeting parents.
- **35% decrease in cost-per-acquisition (CPA)** from optimized ad targeting and refined creative.
- **35K TikTok views and 800 parent referrals** from the influencer campaign's first three weeks.
- **60% completion rate on YouTube ads** targeting parents.
