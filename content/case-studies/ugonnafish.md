## The mission

Ugonnafish is a fishing trip booking platform that connects fishing enthusiasts with professional guides and charter services across various locations. It needed help driving more traffic to its site, increasing bookings, and integrating a seamless payment process for users.

Despite having a user-friendly platform, Ugonnafish struggled to reach its target audience, boost conversions, and offer a smooth payment experience. It also lacked a comprehensive marketing strategy to leverage its online presence.

## The work

Over four months, Get-To-Rev implemented a strategic, multi-channel digital marketing plan. Starting with a Facebook Messenger bot, Instagram ads, influencer collaborations, and an AdWords campaign in month 1, the platform quickly gained traction.

By month 2, these efforts were scaled and optimized. In month 3, SEO work intensified and the influencer campaign expanded, bringing in more organic traffic and bookings. By month 4, Stripe payment integration was fully optimized, and new ad channels and retargeting were explored.

As a result, Ugonnafish saw a 200% increase in bookings, a 250% boost in website traffic, and a 30% decrease in cost-per-acquisition. The Stripe integration reduced friction in checkout, enhancing the user experience.

### Onboarding

- **First online meeting.** Learned about the business, target audience and pain points, including the need for better visibility and a smoother payment experience.
- **Package suggestion.** An integrated marketing plan plus technical support for Stripe payment integration.
- **Roadmap.** Key digital channels to reach potential customers and improve engagement.

### First month: roadmap implementation

- **Facebook Messenger bot.** An automated bot letting users ask questions, get recommendations and book directly through chat.
- **Instagram ads and influencer campaign.** Ads featuring scenic trips and customer stories, plus well-known fishing-community influencers sharing their own experience on the platform.
- **SEO.** Keyword research and content optimization around terms like "guided fishing trips" and "fishing charters near me," plus technical SEO improvements.
- **AdWords.** A highly targeted campaign on high-conversion fishing-trip keywords.
- **Stripe integration.** A seamless, secure payment experience that reduced checkout abandonment.

### Second month and beyond

The Messenger bot and Instagram campaigns were continually A/B tested and budget shifted to top performers. SEO work kept capturing more search traffic, additional influencers were onboarded to reach new segments, and Stripe's checkout experience was refined further.

## Conclusion

Get-To-Rev's strategic approach, combining Facebook Messenger, Instagram marketing, influencer partnerships, SEO and AdWords, allowed Ugonnafish to grow its customer base and streamline the booking process.

**Ongoing partnership.** Ugonnafish continues to partner with Get-To-Rev to optimize its digital marketing and sustain growth.

## Results

- **200% increase in bookings** within the first two months, driven mainly by Messenger and Instagram.
- **250% increase in website traffic** from SEO and AdWords.
- **30% decrease in cost-per-acquisition** from optimized ad spend across channels.
- **10K Instagram influencer engagements** in the campaign's first month.
- **Seamless payment integration**: Stripe led to a 40% decrease in cart abandonment and set the foundation for vertical integration with fishing tour operators.
