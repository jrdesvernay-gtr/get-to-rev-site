## The mission

Lullify is a mobile app designed to help parents put their baby to sleep by playing soothing lullabies. The app offers a variety of sounds and lullabies to promote better sleep for infants, helping parents manage bedtime routines with ease.

Despite the app's potential, Lullify was struggling to attract new users and increase downloads. It needed to build awareness among new parents and optimize its app store presence and marketing channels to boost engagement and app installs.

## The work

Over three months, Get-To-Rev implemented a tailored marketing strategy that significantly increased Lullify's app downloads and visibility.

In month 1, targeted ad campaigns and app store optimization set the foundation for success. By month 2, campaigns were optimized for better performance, while month 3 saw scaling and expansion of the best-performing channels.

With a 150% increase in app downloads and a 200% growth in app store traffic, Lullify is now well-positioned for continued growth, helping parents soothe their babies to sleep more easily.

### Onboarding

- **First online meeting.** Understood Lullify's goals: increasing app downloads, improving app store visibility, and running successful ad campaigns targeting new parents.
- **Package recommendation.** A multi-channel strategy to drive app downloads, increase app store traffic, and optimize ad spend.
- **Roadmap.** A three-month plan built around new-parent prospecting channels, app store optimization, an AdWords app-install campaign, Facebook and Instagram campaigns, and native ads with Outbrain.

### First month: roadmap implementation

- **New parent prospecting.** Channels built around social media and forums, with messaging tailored to first-time parents.
- **App Store Optimization (ASO).** Refined the app description, optimized keywords like "baby sleep" and "lullabies," and updated visuals.
- **AdWords app-install campaigns.** Targeted parents searching for baby sleep solutions, optimized to lower cost-per-install.
- **Facebook and Instagram campaigns.** Ads showing how Lullify helps babies sleep, targeting parents of newborns and toddlers.
- **Native ads with Outbrain.** Native placements on parenting and family websites, driving traffic to the app store page.

### Second and third months: optimization and scaling

Prospecting and ad campaigns were refined toward parents with babies under one year old, with budget scaled toward the highest-converting campaigns. App Store and AdWords listings were further optimized around high-intent search terms, with retargeting added for visitors who didn't install. Facebook and Instagram creative was A/B tested across formats, and Outbrain native ads expanded to more parenting and family sites.

## Conclusion

Get-To-Rev's strategic approach, combining new-parent prospecting, App Store optimization, AdWords campaigns, Facebook and Instagram marketing, and native ads with Outbrain, allowed Lullify to expand its user base and significantly increase app downloads.

**Ongoing partnership.** Lullify continues to work with Get-To-Rev to optimize its marketing efforts and drive sustained growth in app downloads and user engagement.

## Results

- **150% increase in app downloads**, driven by the multi-channel strategy within three months.
- **200% increase in app store traffic** from App Store Optimization and targeted ad campaigns.
- **28% decrease in cost-per-install (CPI)** from ongoing AdWords and Facebook optimization.
- **High engagement from native ads**, with Outbrain driving consistent app downloads from parenting websites.
