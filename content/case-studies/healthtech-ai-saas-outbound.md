## The mission

Get-To-Rev helped an AI-powered clinical research platform modernize its outbound efforts and reduce manual sales workload, using a data-driven, automated stack for scalable, personalized prospecting ahead of its Series A funding round.

The client specializes in streamlining and modernizing data and study management for life sciences and healthcare research organizations. It aimed to speed up partnership and client acquisition ahead of its Series A by targeting biotech, biopharma, CRO and molecular diagnostics companies with its flagship AI platform, while modernizing outbound efforts and reducing manual sales workload.

## The work

In 16 weeks, Get-To-Rev executed a comprehensive ABM strategy built around Clay, HubSpot and Reply.io.

Weeks 1-4 focused on sourcing, ICP mapping and a messaging foundation: building and personalizing the first 200 leads in Clay across all target segments, and setting up lead scoring in HubSpot.

Weeks 5-9 rolled out outreach automation: continuing LinkedIn connection growth to 20 a day, launching Reply.io campaigns, and syncing lead engagement automatically into HubSpot lifecycle stages.

Weeks 10-13 activated campaign segmentation: Solution vs. Webinar campaigns, LinkedIn and Facebook video ads, retargeting across every platform, and automated lifecycle progression from lead to MQL, SQL, engaged and opportunity.

Weeks 14-16 optimized performance: RLSA switched to direct targeting, and sales handoff automation added with instant rep alerts and SDR/AE assignment for meeting bookers and webinar SQLs.

### Strategy

- **First online meeting.** Proposed launching and automating outbound outreach on Clay, HubSpot and Reply.io, targeting life science companies (biotech, biopharma, CRO, molecular diagnostics) to grow the pipeline and book meetings with high-fit accounts.
- **Package.** The ABM package: the Clay/Reply/HubSpot tech stack, plus paid ads on LinkedIn, Google Search (RLSA) and Meta.
- **Automation tech stack.** Clay for lead research and enrichment; LinkedIn Sales Navigator for ICP prospecting and targeting; Reply.io for cold outreach and sequencing; Zapier for workflow automation across tools; HubSpot for CRM, lead scoring, nurturing and retention.
- **Target audience.** Biotech, biopharma, CRO, diagnostics and molecular population database centers.
- **Goals.** Book meetings with key ICP decision-makers; generate MQLs and SQLs; grow qualified pipeline; nurture and convert leads across funnel stages.

### Cold outreach

**Clay lead sourcing.** Targeted lead research and enrichment for biotech, biopharma, CRO and molecular diagnostics companies, with AI-powered ICP mapping, company classification and personalized messaging generation.

**Reply.io sequencing.** Automated email outreach with Solution Campaign sequences (demo booking) and Webinar Campaign sequences (event registration), integrated with LinkedIn connection requests and automated positive-reply detection.

**HubSpot automation.** CRM management with predictive lead scoring, automated lifecycle progression, nurturing workflows, and sales handoff automation with instant rep alerts and SDR/AE assignment.

### Retargeting

**Google Search Ads (RLSA).** HubSpot audiences fed into AdWords to retarget Clay-sourced leads when they searched for a similar solution.

**LinkedIn Ads.** The same HubSpot/Clay list retargeted on LinkedIn.

**Meta Ads.** Added a custom-audience layer, built from Clay leads, on top of the client's existing Facebook retargeting of website visitors.

## What we learned

**Model cost.** Processing large volumes of records with AI (classifying ICPs, analyzing funding and revenue, sourcing news for messaging) gets expensive. Clients create their own OpenAI and Gemini API keys, which costs less than relying solely on Clay's built-in AI. For high-reasoning tasks like business-model analysis and ICP segmentation, a cheaper model screens first (for example, keyword detection) and a stronger model like Claude or GPT-5 analyzes only low-confidence records.

**Company verification.** Clay often includes records for companies with high default rates or recent acquisitions, so each company's website is checked for active status before outreach.

**Founder involvement.** LinkedIn connect and reply rates improved significantly when a founder personally took part in Reply.io outreach — active leadership involvement increased top-of-funnel success and accelerated down-funnel conversions.

## Conclusion

With 3,000 primary buyers across 1,800 companies contacted and industry-beating conversion rates, the client is positioned for accelerated growth with a scalable, automated outbound system.

## Results

Key metrics:

- **3,000 primary buyers** contacted across 1,800 companies.
- **LinkedIn connect rate: 12%.**
- **LinkedIn reply rate: 11.8%** (of connections).
- **Email open rate: 32%.**
- **Email reply rate: 4.1%** (complex SaaS, healthcare).
- **Meetings booked: 0.7%** of total sequence contacts (21 meetings).
- **Client signups: 0.15%** of total sequence contacts by week 16 (5 clients).
