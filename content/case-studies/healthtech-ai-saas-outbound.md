## Challenge

The client needed to speed up partnership and client acquisition before its Series A and cut the manual work in sales, with outreach that was personalized and scalable.

## Approach

1. **Weeks 1-4: foundation.** Sourcing, ICP mapping and messaging. The first 200 leads built and personalized in Clay across all target segments, with lead scoring set up in HubSpot.
2. **Weeks 5-9: outreach automation.** LinkedIn connection growth to 20 a day, Reply.io campaigns live, and engagement synced automatically into HubSpot lifecycle stages.
3. **Weeks 10-13: segmentation and retargeting.** Solution and webinar campaigns, LinkedIn and Facebook video ads, retargeting on every platform, and automated progression from lead to MQL, SQL, engaged and opportunity.
4. **Weeks 14-16: optimization and handoff.** RLSA switched to direct targeting, and sales handoff automation added with instant rep alerts and SDR or AE assignment.

## What we learned

**Model cost.** AI classification across thousands of records gets expensive. Clients create their own OpenAI and Gemini API keys, which costs less than Clay's built-in AI. A cheaper model screens first and a stronger model analyzes only low-confidence records.

**Company verification.** Enrichment includes companies that have defaulted or been acquired, so each company's website is checked before outreach.

**Founder involvement.** LinkedIn connection and reply rates improved when a founder took part in the outreach personally.
